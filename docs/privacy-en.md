# Privacy Policy - Euro Inline

[Deutsch](/euro-inline/privacy/) | **English** | [Français](/euro-inline/privacy/fr/) | [Español](/euro-inline/privacy/es/) | [日本語](/euro-inline/privacy/ja/)

Euro Inline processes visible website content locally in your browser to detect currency amounts and display converted prices directly on the website.

Website content, product names, and prices are not transmitted to the developer or stored permanently. Euro Inline does not collect browsing history or personal information.

To calculate exchange rates, Euro Inline requests data from the Frankfurter API. Only the required source and target currencies are transmitted, for example JPY and EUR.

Extension settings and cached exchange rates are stored locally in your browser.

Euro Inline does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services and does not sell user data.
